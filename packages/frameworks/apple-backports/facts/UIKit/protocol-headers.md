## Why the table overrides some members of UITabBarControllerSidebarDelegate

The snapshot (`spec/UIKit/protocols.tsv`, from the 16.4 SDK) does not hold four things the committed header wrote, so
`spec/protocol-headers.tsv` carries them as `replace` and `insert` rows after the protocol's `bare` row:

- `__kindof UITab *` in the willBeginDisplayingTab, didEndDisplayingTab and the three context-menu and swipe members: the
  snapshot's declaration text took `__kindof` out, and the committed header has it. Measured: the header's clang dump
  (`-ast-print`, against iPhoneOS16.4.sdk) shows `__kindof`, and the snapshot's text does not.
- `API_AVAILABLE(ios(18.4))` on the drag-session and drop members: the 16.4 SDK does not declare the protocol (the snapshot has
  `-` in its in16 column for every member), and the committed
  header declares the members as written.
- `sidebarAvailabilityDidChange:`, `API_AVAILABLE(ios(27.0))`: a member the 16.4 SDK does not declare, placed first in the
  body as the committed header has it.
