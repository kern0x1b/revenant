## Why the body keeps its nullability

MTL4CommandQueue is a `body-keep` row: its members keep the `_Nonnull` and `_Nullable` the snapshot holds, as the committed
header wrote them. Measured: without it the `commit:` members' pointer types differ in the clang dump.
